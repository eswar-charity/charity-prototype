Guest Flow
Yes, guests can see nonprofits — but only in view-only, "story" mode. Here's exactly what the blueprint specifies:

What guests can see about nonprofits

From the document's own list of what a Guest can do: view the feed, open public event pages, view nonprofit story sections, and share public links. Nonprofit story sections are explicitly named as one of the five things a guest is allowed to see without any identity gate.

In practice, this shows up in Journey B (Public Event Page Viewing): when a guest opens an event page, they see "the event, the nonprofit context, social proof, and participants." The nonprofit isn't just a name tag on the event — its story, mission context, and social proof render right there for an anonymous visitor, because the design intent is that the event page "should feel like a story moment, not a donation form."

What guests cannot do regarding nonprofits

Anything that requires identity is gated — following, saving a nonprofit, joining anything connected to them, donating to them, or accessing any nonprofit-facing dashboard (obviously, since that's the Launchpad, restricted to the NP Admin role entirely). The moment a guest tries to do any of those, the identity gate fires with contextual copy explaining why, and after signup they're routed back to the exact action they attempted.

The boundary, stated plainly

Discovery of nonprofits — browsing their story, seeing their mission, seeing the events they're connected to — is fully open to guests, by design, because it's part of the platform's core "voyeur-friendly" discovery loop. Anything that turns that discovery into a relationship (follow, donate, join, save) requires becoming an authenticated user first.

So the honest one-line answer: guests can see nonprofits' public story and event presence, but cannot interact with, follow, or donate to them without signing up.
